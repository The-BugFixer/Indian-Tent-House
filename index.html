Common enquiries

Frequently asked questions.

How far in advance should we reserve Indian Tent House for a wedding?

For peak winter and wedding season (November to March) we suggest booking 2 to 4 months ahead. For smaller functions, 3 to 7 days is often enough, depending on availability.

Can your hangars withstand sudden rain and high winds?

Yes. Our aluminium hangars use weatherproof covering and strong frames, and our team inspects the ground and anchoring at your venue before setup.

Do you provide complete turnkey services including lighting, gensets and stage decor?

Yes. We handle tents, mandap and stage decor, lighting, furniture and generator backup as one package.

What information is needed to receive a customised quotation?

Share the event type, date, expected guests and venue location. We will schedule a free site survey and send a quote.
